Reflection

What is it, how it works and which results is possible to get.

My first semester was full of steep learning curves and exceptionally tight deadlines for actual practice. We were shown so many incredible things we could create, but to achieve such complex results, it is essential to take it one step at a time rather than setting a massive goal without knowing how to approach it. As I mentioned earlier, I would have been much happier if we were given more time for pure experimentation, without the pressure of presenting every single exercise as a polished project.

Because we had to showcase our work every time, I was incredibly hard on myself; to me, presentation meant that I had to deliver my absolute best since it would be displayed in front of others. Had it been a low-stakes learning practice, I would have felt free to try so many different techniques without the burden of labelling each attempt as a formal project. Only after accumulating that practical foundation would I naturally come up with a vision and a solid plan for a final piece, already fully aware of what I am capable of achieving.

If I had more time and better health, I would have truly loved to do more. However, that is the reality I am currently navigating, and I suppose I have to accept things as they are.
